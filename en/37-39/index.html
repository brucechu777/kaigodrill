Exam 37 · 第37回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 39 問39

次つぎの記述きじゅつのうち，2019年ねん（令和元年れいわがんねん）の認知症施策推進大綱にんちしょうせさくすいしんたいこうに関かんする説明せつめいとして，最もっとも適切てきせつなものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. 「共生きょうせい」と「予防よぼう」を車くるまの両輪りょうりんとして施策せさくを推進すいしんしていく。
  2. 「予防よぼう」とは，「認知症にんちしょう（dementia）にならない」という意味いみである。
  3. 「認知症高齢者等にんちしょうこうれいしゃとうにやさしい地域ちいきづくり」を推進すいしんする7つの柱はしらが示しめされた。
  4. 「普及啓発ふきゅうけいはつ・本人発信支援ほんにんはっしんしえん」として，家族かぞくが積極的せっきょくてきに本人ほんにんの意思いしを代弁だいべんすることが示しめされた。
  5. 策定後さくていごは，毎年施策まいとしせさくの進捗しんちょくを確認かくにんすることが示しめされた。

Translation

Among the following statements about the National Framework for Dementia Policy Promotion (認知症施策推進大綱) of 2019 (Reiwa 1), choose the most appropriate one.

  1. The policy moves forward with 'living together' (共生) and 'prevention' (予防) as its two main wheels.
  2. 'Prevention' (予防) means 'not getting dementia (認知症).'
  3. It set out 7 pillars for building communities that are kind to older people with dementia (認知症) and others.
  4. Under 'raising awareness and supporting the person's own message' (普及啓発・本人発信支援), it says that family members should actively speak for the person's wishes.
  5. After the plan was made, it says the progress of the policy should be checked every year.
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 1

Why 1 is correct

The National Framework for Dementia Policy Promotion moves forward with 'living together' (共生) and 'prevention' (予防) as its two main wheels. 'Living together' means that people with dementia (認知症) can keep living in the community they know well. 'Prevention' means delaying the start of dementia. This idea was carried on in the Basic Plan for Dementia Policy Promotion, decided by the Cabinet in December 2024 (Reiwa 6).

Why 2 is wrong

In the Framework, 'prevention' does not mean 'not getting dementia (認知症).' It means 'delaying the start of dementia' or 'slowing it down after it starts.'

Why 3 is wrong

The 7 pillars for 'building communities that are kind to older people with dementia (認知症) and others' were set out in the New Orange Plan (Comprehensive Strategy to Promote Dementia Policies) of 2015 (Heisei 27). The 2019 Framework has 5 pillars.

Why 4 is wrong

'Support for the person's own message' means helping the person with dementia (認知症) express their own thoughts in their own words. It does not mean family members speaking for them.

Why 5 is wrong

The Framework covers the period up to 2025 (Reiwa 7), and it says progress should be checked at around the 3-year mark, not every year.

Key point

The Framework for Dementia Policy Promotion (認知症施策推進大綱) has "living together" (共生) and "prevention" as its two wheels.

Key terms

認知症施策推進大綱にんちしょうせさくすいしんたいこう
A document made by the government in 2019 that shows the direction of dementia (認知症) policy.
共生きょうせい
Being able to keep living together in the same community, even after getting dementia (認知症).
本人発信支援ほんにんはっしんしえん
Helping the person with dementia (認知症) share their own thoughts and wishes in their own words.
