Exam 37 · 第37回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 41 問41

認知症にんちしょう（dementia）の高齢者こうれいしゃにみられる，せん妄もうに関かんする記述きじゅつとして，適てき切せつなものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. 覚醒かくせいレベルが重度じゅうどに低下ていかした状態じょうたいである。
  2. 症状しょうじょうの変動へんどうが少すくないことが特徴とくちょうである。
  3. 夜間やかんよりも日中にっちゅうに生しょうじやすいことが特徴とくちょうである。
  4. 認知機能障害にんちきのうしょうがいがみられることはまれである。
  5. 関与かんよする因子いんしを特定とくていすることが重要じゅうようである。

Translation

Among the following statements about delirium (せん妄) seen in older people with dementia (認知症), choose the appropriate one.

  1. It is a state where the level of alertness (覚醒) has dropped severely.
  2. A feature is that the symptoms change very little.
  3. A feature is that it happens more easily in the daytime than at night.
  4. It is rare to see cognitive impairment (認知機能障害).
  5. It is important to find the factors that are involved.
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 5

Why 5 is correct

Delirium (せん妄) can be caused or triggered by things like dehydration, medicine, infection, pain, or a change in surroundings. Finding and removing these factors is the most important response.

Why 1 is wrong

Delirium (せん妄) is a state of light clouding of consciousness (a mild consciousness disorder). A severe drop in alertness (覚醒), where the person does not wake up, is a different state, such as a coma.

Why 2 is wrong

A feature of delirium (せん妄) is that symptoms change a lot, getting better or worse depending on the time of day. Little change in symptoms is more typical of dementia (認知症).

Why 3 is wrong

Delirium (せん妄) happens more easily at night, and this is called 'nighttime delirium' (夜間せん妄). More care is needed at night than in the daytime.

Why 4 is wrong

In delirium (せん妄), cognitive impairment (認知機能障害) is often seen, such as not being able to keep attention, or talking in a way that does not make sense. It is not rare.

Key point

For delirium (せん妄), it is important to find and remove the causes.

Key terms

せん妄せんもう
A mild consciousness disorder that starts suddenly. It can show as restlessness, hallucinations, or not making sense, for a short time.
覚醒かくせい
Being awake and aware of what is around you.
認知機能障害にんちきのうしょうがい
Cognitive impairment: a drop in brain functions like memory, attention, and judgment.
