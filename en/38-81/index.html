Exam 38 · 第38回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 81 問81

次つぎの記述きじゅつのうち，認知症にんちしょう（dementia）のある人ひとにみられる妄想もうそうの説明せつめいとして，最もっとも適切てきせつなものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. 自分じぶんでしまい忘わすれた物ものが見みつからず，誰だれかが盗ぬすんだと思おもい込こむのは，嫉妬妄想しっともうそうである。
  2. 孫まごと会あって話はなしをしたにもかかわらず，孫まごにずっと会あわせてもらえていないと主しゅ張ちょうするのは，カプグラ症候群しょうこうぐんである。
  3. 介護支援専門員かいごしえんせんもんいん（ケアマネジャー）と妻つまが話はなしているのを見みて，妻つまの浮気相手うわきあいてだと思おもい込こむのは，幻まぼろしの同居人どうきょにんである。
  4. 家族かぞくなど身近みぢかな人ひとがよく似にた偽物にせものと入いれ替かわっていると主張しゅちょうするのは，被害妄ひがいもう想そうである。
  5. 身体的疾患しんたいてきしっかんがないにもかかわらず，自分じぶんが病気びょうきだと思おもい込こむのは，心気妄想しんきもうそうである。

Translation

Which of the following is the most appropriate description of a delusion seen in people with dementia (認知症)? Choose one.

  1. When a person cannot find something they put away themselves and believes someone stole it, this is a delusion of jealousy (嫉妬妄想).
  2. When a person talks with their grandchild but insists they have not been allowed to see the grandchild for a long time, this is Capgras syndrome (カプグラ症候群).
  3. When a person sees their wife talking with the care manager (ケアマネジャー) and believes the care manager is the wife's lover, this is phantom boarder syndrome (幻の同居人).
  4. When a person insists that a family member or someone close has been replaced by a similar-looking impostor, this is a persecutory delusion (被害妄想).
  5. When a person has no physical illness but believes they are sick, this is a delusion of illness (心気妄想).
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 5

Why 5 is correct

A person with no physical illness who strongly believes they have a serious illness has a delusion of illness (心気妄想). So answer 5 is correct.

Why 1 is wrong

Believing that someone stole something you put away yourself is called a 'delusion of theft' (物盗られ妄想), a type of persecutory delusion (被害妄想). A delusion of jealousy (嫉妬妄想) is believing that your spouse is having an affair.

Why 2 is wrong

This happens because of a memory disorder (記憶障害) — forgetting that you met and talked with your grandchild. It is not Capgras syndrome (カプグラ症候群). Capgras syndrome is believing that someone close to you has been replaced by a similar-looking impostor.

Why 3 is wrong

Believing your wife is having an affair is a delusion of jealousy (嫉妬妄想). Phantom boarder syndrome (幻の同居人) is believing that someone who does not live in the house is living there with you.

Why 4 is wrong

Believing that someone close to you has been replaced by an impostor is Capgras syndrome (カプグラ症候群). A persecutory delusion (被害妄想) is believing that you are being harmed, for example that something was stolen from you or that people are saying bad things about you.

Key point

Believing you are ill when your body has no illness is delusion of illness (心気妄想).

Key terms

心気妄想しんきもうそう
A delusion of believing you are sick when you have no illness.
嫉妬妄想しっともうそう
A delusion of believing your spouse is having an affair.
被害妄想ひがいもうそう
A delusion of believing others are harming you. Delusion of theft is one type.
カプグラ症候群カプグラしょうこうぐん
A condition of believing someone close to you has been replaced by a similar-looking impostor.
