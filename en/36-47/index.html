Exam 36 · 第36回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 47 問47

Ｍさん（80歳さい，女性じょせい，要介護ようかいご1）は，アルツハイマー型認知症がたにんちしょう（dementia of the Alzheimer’s type）であり，3日前かまえに認知症対応型共同生活介護にんちしょうたいおうがたきょうどうせいかつかいご（認知症高齢にんちしょうこうれい者しゃグループホーム）に入居にゅうきょした。主治医しゅじいから向精神薬こうせいしんやくが処方しょほうされている。居室きょしつでは穏おだやかに過すごしていた。夕食後ゆうしょくご，表情ひょうじょうが険けわしくなり，「こんなところにはいられません。私わたしは家いえに帰かえります」と大声おおごえを上あげ，ほかの利用者りようしゃにも，「あなたも一緒いっしょに帰かえりましょう」と声こえをかけて皆みなが落おち着つかなくなることがあった。Ｍさんの介護かいごを検討けんとうするときに優先ゆうせんすることとして，最もっとも適切てきせつなものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. Ｍさんが訴うったえている内容ないよう
  2. Ｍさんの日中にっちゅうの過すごし方かた
  3. ほかの利用者りようしゃが落おち着つかなくなったこと
  4. 対応たいおうに困こまったこと
  5. 薬くすりが効きかなかったこと

Translation

Ms. M (80 years old, female, care level 1) has dementia of the Alzheimer's type, and moved into a group home for people with dementia (認知症対応型共同生活介護／認知症高齢者グループホーム) 3 days ago. Her primary doctor has prescribed a psychotropic medication. She had been spending time calmly in her room. After dinner, her expression became tense, and she shouted loudly, "I can't stay in a place like this. I'm going home," and also called out to other users (利用者), "Come home with me too," which made everyone unsettled. Among the following, choose the 1 option that is the most appropriate priority to consider when planning care for Ms. M.

  1. What Ms. M is saying (the content of her complaint)
  2. How Ms. M spends her daytime
  3. The fact that other users became unsettled
  4. The fact that staff had trouble responding
  5. The fact that the medication did not work
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 1

Why 1 is correct

The first thing to consider is what Ms. M is saying — "I'm going home" — and the feelings behind those words. Only 3 days after moving in, amid this change of environment, understanding what Ms. M is anxious about is the starting point for planning her care.

Why 2 is wrong

Arranging how she spends her daytime is also important, but that comes after understanding the meaning of what she is saying. Deciding on measures first, without this, leaves Ms. M's own feelings out of the picture.

Why 3 is wrong

The fact that other users became unsettled is a problem for the facility staff, and does not by itself help in understanding Ms. M herself. Accepting what Ms. M is saying comes first.

Why 4 is wrong

The fact that staff had trouble responding is the caregivers' point of view. Focusing on the caregivers' difficulty makes it harder to see Ms. M's own anxiety.

Why 5 is wrong

Since it is only the 3rd day after moving in, it is natural to think the change of environment is the cause. Concluding right away that the medication did not work is not appropriate — evaluating medication is the doctor's job.

Key point

Care starts by understanding what the person says and how she feels.

Key terms

認知症対応型共同生活介護にんちしょうたいおうがたきょうどうせいかつかいご
A group home for older people with dementia. A service where a small number of residents live together and receive care.
向精神薬こうせいしんやく
Medication that acts on mood or mental state. It is prescribed by a doctor.
