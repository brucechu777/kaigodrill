Exam 36 · 第36回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 39 問39

高齢者こうれいしゃの自動車運転免許じどうしゃうんてんめんきょに関かんする次つぎの記述きじゅつのうち，正ただしいものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. 75歳さいから免許更新時めんきょこうしんじの認知機能検査にんちきのうけんさが義務ぎむづけられている。
  2. 80歳さいから免許更新時めんきょこうしんじの運転技能検査うんてんぎのうけんさが義務ぎむづけられている。
  3. 軽度認知障害けいどにんちしょうがい（mild cognitive impairment）と診断しんだんされた人ひとは運転免許取消うんてんめんきょとりけしになる。
  4. 認知症にんちしょう（dementia）の人ひとはサポートカー限定免許げんていめんきょであれば運転うんてんが可能かのうである。
  5. 認知症にんちしょう（dementia）による運転免許取消うんてんめんきょとりけしの後あと，運転経歴証明書うんてんけいれきしょうめいしょが交付こうふされる。

（注ちゅう）「サポートカー限定免許げんていめんきょ」とは，道路交通法第どうろこうつうほうだい91条じょうの2の規定きていに基もとづく条件じょうけんが付ふされた免許めんきょのことである。

Translation

Among the following statements about driving licenses for older drivers, choose the 1 option that is correct.

  1. A cognitive function test is required when renewing a license from age 75 onward.
  2. A driving skills test is required when renewing a license from age 80 onward.
  3. A person diagnosed with mild cognitive impairment (MCI) has their driving license revoked.
  4. A person with dementia can drive if they have a safety-support-car-only license.
  5. After a driving license is revoked because of dementia, a driving history certificate is issued.

(Note) A "safety-support-car-only license" is a license with a condition attached based on Article 91-2 of the Road Traffic Act.

Show answer and explanation

Answer: 1

Why 1 is correct

By law, people aged 75 and over must take a cognitive function test when they renew their driving license. If this test suggests a risk of dementia, the person then has to see a doctor for a diagnosis.

Why 2 is wrong

The driving skills test is not required starting "from age 80." It applies only to people aged 75 and over who have committed certain violations, such as running a red light or driving the wrong way.

Why 3 is wrong

Mild cognitive impairment (MCI) is not dementia, so being diagnosed with it alone does not cause a license to be revoked. A license is revoked when a person is diagnosed with dementia.

Why 4 is wrong

A safety-support-car-only license is a license with the condition that the person may only drive a car equipped with things like brake-assist devices. It is not a system that lets a person with dementia drive just because the car has safety devices.

Why 5 is wrong

A driving history certificate is issued to a person who gives up their license voluntarily. If a license is revoked because of dementia, this is not giving it up voluntarily, so the certificate is not issued.

Key point

People aged 75 or older take a cognitive function test when renewing their driver's license.

Key terms

認知機能検査にんちきのうけんさ
A test taken when renewing a license, which checks memory and orientation to time.
運転技能検査うんてんぎのうけんさ
A test that checks driving skill by actually driving a car. It is taken by older drivers with a record of certain violations.
サポートカー限定免許サポートカーげんていめんきょ
A license with the condition that the person may only drive a car equipped with safety devices.
運転経歴証明書うんてんけいれきしょうめいしょ
A certificate given to a person who has voluntarily given up their license. It can also be used as ID.
