Exam 37 · 第37回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 43 問43

次つぎのうち，認知症にんちしょう（dementia）のリスクを高たかめる要因よういんとして，最もっとも適切てきせつなものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. 身体活動しんたいかつどう
  2. 不飽和脂肪酸ふほうわしぼうさんの摂取せっしゅ
  3. 歯はがなくなることによる咀嚼機能そしゃくきのうの低下ていか
  4. 難聴なんちょうによる補聴器ほちょうきの使用しよう
  5. ボランティア活動かつどう

Translation

Which of the following increases the risk of dementia (認知症)? Choose the most appropriate one.

  1. Physical activity
  2. Eating unsaturated fatty acids
  3. A drop in chewing function (咀嚼機能) because of losing teeth
  4. Using a hearing aid because of hearing loss (難聴)
  5. Volunteer activity
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 3

Why 3 is correct

When teeth are lost and chewing function (咀嚼機能) drops, there is less stimulation to the brain, and nutrition can become unbalanced. This raises the risk of dementia (認知症). Treating teeth or using dentures so a person can chew again helps prevent this.

Why 1 is wrong

Physical activity, like walking or moving the body, is a factor that lowers the risk of dementia (認知症). It does not increase it.

Why 2 is wrong

Eating unsaturated fatty acids, which are found a lot in fish, is thought to lower the risk, not raise it.

Why 4 is wrong

It is hearing loss (難聴) itself that raises the risk. Using a hearing aid to hear better lowers the risk, because it helps a person keep having conversations with others.

Why 5 is wrong

Social activity, like volunteer work, gives more chances to talk with people, so it lowers the risk of dementia (認知症).

Key point

When chewing ability drops, the risk of dementia (認知症) goes up.

Key terms

咀嚼そしゃく
Chewing food with the teeth.
不飽和脂肪酸ふほうわしぼうさん
A type of fat found a lot in fish and plant oil. It is thought to be good for blood vessel health.
難聴なんちょう
Hearing loss: having trouble hearing.
