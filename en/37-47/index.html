Exam 37 · 第37回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 47 問47

次つぎの記述きじゅつのうち，認知症疾患医療にんちしょうしっかんいりょうセンターの説明せつめいとして，適切てきせつなものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. 事業じぎょうの実施主体じっししゅたいは，市町村しちょうそんである。
  2. 都道府県とどうふけんごとに，1か所しょの設置せっちが義務ぎむづけられている。
  3. 認知症にんちしょう（dementia）の鑑別診断かんべつしんだんを行おこなう。
  4. 主おもに認知症にんちしょう（dementia）が進行しんこうした人ひとの入院治療にゅういんちりょうを行おこなう。
  5. 介護保険法かいごほけんほうに定さだめられている。

Translation

Which of the following describes a Medical Center for Dementia (認知症疾患医療センター)? Choose the appropriate one.

  1. The body that runs this service is the municipality (city, town, or village).
  2. Each prefecture must set up exactly one center.
  3. It carries out differential diagnosis (鑑別診断) of dementia (認知症).
  4. It mainly gives hospital treatment to people whose dementia (認知症) has progressed.
  5. It is set out in the Long-Term Care Insurance Act.
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 3

Why 3 is correct

A Medical Center for Dementia (認知症疾患医療センター) has specialist doctors who examine a person and carry out differential diagnosis (鑑別診断) — deciding whether the person has dementia (認知症), and if so, which type. It also gives specialist medical consultation and responds when symptoms suddenly get worse.

Why 1 is wrong

The body that runs this service is the prefecture and the designated city, not the municipality.

Why 2 is wrong

There is no rule that each prefecture must have exactly one center. Centers are set up at a rate of about one per secondary medical area, and there are about 500 centers across the country.

Why 4 is wrong

The main role is not hospital treatment, but differential diagnosis (鑑別診断) and specialist medical consultation. Hospital treatment is given when there is also a physical illness, or when symptoms are severe.

Why 5 is wrong

It is not set out in the Long-Term Care Insurance Act. It is a service called the 'Medical Center for Dementia Operation Project' (認知症疾患医療センター運営事業), which started in fiscal year 2008 (Heisei 20), run by prefectures and designated cities.

Key point

Specialist doctors at a Medical Center for Dementia (認知症疾患医療センター) carry out differential diagnosis (鑑別診断).

Key terms

認知症疾患医療センターにんちしょうしっかんいりょうセンター
Medical Center for Dementia: a medical institution that gives specialist diagnosis and consultation for dementia (認知症).
鑑別診断かんべつしんだん
Doing tests to work out which illness it is, or which type of dementia (認知症) it is.
実施主体じっししゅたい
The body responsible for running the service.
