Exam 36 · 第36回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 45 問45

認知機能障害にんちきのうしょうがいによる生活せいかつへの影響えいきょうに関かんする記述きじゅつとして，最もっとも適切てきせつなものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. 遂行機能障害すいこうきのうしょうがいにより，自宅じたくがわからない。
  2. 記憶障害きおくしょうがいにより，出だされた食事しょくじを食たべない。
  3. 相貌失認そうぼうしつにんにより，目めの前まえの家族かぞくがわからない。
  4. 視空間認知障害しくうかんにんちしょうがいにより，今日きょうの日付ひづけがわからない。
  5. 病識低下びょうしきていかにより，うつ状態じょうたいになりやすい。

Translation

Among the following, choose the 1 option that is the most appropriate description of how cognitive impairment affects daily life.

  1. Because of executive function disorder, the person does not recognize their own home.
  2. Because of memory disorder, the person does not eat the meal that is served.
  3. Because of prosopagnosia (face blindness), the person does not recognize a family member right in front of them.
  4. Because of visuospatial cognitive impairment, the person does not know today's date.
  5. Because of reduced awareness of illness, the person is prone to becoming depressed.
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 3

Why 3 is correct

Prosopagnosia is a disorder in which a person cannot tell who someone is even when looking at their face. Because of this, a person may not recognize a family member who is right in front of them.

Why 1 is wrong

Not recognizing one's own home is disorientation to place. Executive function disorder is being unable to plan and carry out things in the right order — for example, no longer being able to work out the steps of cooking.

Why 2 is wrong

Not eating a meal that has been served can happen because of agnosia — not recognizing that it is food. With memory disorder, what typically happens instead is forgetting that one has already eaten, and trying to eat again and again.

Why 4 is wrong

Not knowing today's date is disorientation to time. Visuospatial cognitive impairment is being unable to grasp the position or distance of objects — for example, being unable to put an arm through a sleeve, or getting lost on a familiar route.

Why 5 is wrong

Reduced awareness of illness is being unable to recognize that one is ill. Because a person cannot recognize this, they are actually less likely to become depressed — becoming depressed is more common in the early stage, when awareness of the illness is still present.

Key point

Prosopagnosia (相貌失認) means not knowing who someone is even when you see the face.

Key terms

遂行機能障害すいこうきのうしょうがい
Being unable to make a plan and carry it out in the right order.
相貌失認そうぼうしつにん
Being unable to tell who someone is, even when looking at their face.
視空間認知障害しくうかんにんちしょうがい
Being unable to grasp the position, distance, and shape relationships of objects.
病識低下びょうしきていか
A weakening of the awareness that one is ill.
