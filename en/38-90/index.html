Exam 38 · 第38回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 90 問90

Ａさん（88歳さい，女性じょせい）は，4年前ねんまえに，認知症にんちしょう（dementia）と診断しんだんされた。在ざい宅たくで長男ちょうなんが介護かいごしている。Ａさんは，3か月前げつまえから，何度なんどもトイレ以外いがいで排泄はいせつしようとする様子ようすがみられた。長男ちょうなんが繰くり返かえしトイレの場所ばしょを教おしえているが，状態じょうたいは変かわらない。長男ちょうなんはＡさんに対たいして，「何回なんかい，同おなじことを言いったら，わかるんだ」と大声おおごえでどなってしまい，その後あとで落おち込こむようになった。また，長男ちょうなんは，「認知症にんちしょう（dementia）がいつまで続つづくのか」「自分じぶんに何なにか問題もんだいがあったのではないか」と言いって，自分じぶんを強つよく責せめている。次つぎのうち，Ａさんの認知症にんちしょう（dementia）を長男ちょうなんが受うけ入いれる過程かていと考かんがえた場合ばあい，このときの長男ちょうなんの心理的特徴しんりてきとくちょうに該当がいとうする段階だんかいとして，最もっとも適切てきせつなものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. ショック期き
  2. 否認期ひにんき
  3. 混乱期こんらんき
  4. 解決かいけつへの努力期どりょくき
  5. 受容期じゅようき

Translation

Ms. A (88 years old, female) was diagnosed with dementia (認知症) 4 years ago. Her eldest son cares for her at home. Since 3 months ago, Ms. A has often tried toileting (排泄) somewhere other than the toilet. Her son has repeatedly shown her where the toilet is, but nothing has changed. Her son has shouted at her, saying, 'How many times do I have to tell you before you understand?' and has felt down afterward. He also blames himself strongly, saying things like, 'How long will this dementia (認知症) go on?' and 'Was there something wrong with me?' If this is seen as the process by which the son comes to accept Ms. A's dementia (認知症), which of the following stages matches his psychological state at this time? Choose the most appropriate one.

  1. Shock stage (ショック期)
  2. Denial stage (否認期)
  3. Confusion stage (混乱期)
  4. Effort-toward-solution stage (解決への努力期)
  5. Acceptance stage (受容期)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 3

Why 3 is correct

The confusion stage (混乱期) is when a person feels anger and sadness about a situation they cannot control, and feels down or blames themselves. The son felt down after shouting and blames himself strongly, so answer 3 is correct.

Why 1 is wrong

The shock stage (ショック期) is right after receiving the diagnosis, when a person feels strong shock and cannot keep up emotionally. It has been 4 years since the diagnosis, so the son is not in this stage.

Why 2 is wrong

The denial stage (否認期) is when a person will not accept that it is dementia. The son is worried on the assumption that the dementia will continue, so he is not in denial.

Why 4 is wrong

The effort-toward-solution stage (解決への努力期) is when a person moves past anger and self-blame and starts thinking positively about what they can do. The son is still blaming himself.

Why 5 is wrong

The acceptance stage (受容期) is when a person accepts the dementia as it is and has gotten used to their new life. The son has not yet accepted it.

Key point

The confusion stage (混乱期) brings anger and sadness, feeling down and blaming oneself.

Key terms

混乱期こんらんき
The stage when strong anger, sadness, and self-blame appear.
否認期ひにんき
The stage of not accepting an illness or disability.
受容期じゅようき
The stage of accepting an illness or disability as it is.
排泄はいせつ
Toileting: passing urine or stool out of the body.
