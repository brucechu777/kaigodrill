Exam 38 · 第38回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 83 問83

次つぎの記述きじゅつのうち，認知症にんちしょう（dementia）の行動こうどう・心理症状しんりしょうじょう（ＢＰＳＤ）に該当がいとうするものとして，最もっとも適切てきせつなものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. 10分前ぷんまえに施設職員しせつしょくいんが挨拶あいさつしに来きたことを，覚おぼえていない。
  2. 病院びょういんにいるのに，自宅じたくにいると思おもっている。
  3. 通所介護つうしょかいご（デイサービス）を利用中りようちゅうに，出口でぐちを探さがして歩あるき回まわる。
  4. 薬くすりを処方しょほうされても，服薬管理ふくやくかんりができない。
  5. 入浴にゅうよくを促うながすと，湯ゆにつかるだけで出でてくる。

Translation

Which of the following is an example of behavioral and psychological symptoms of dementia (行動・心理症状／BPSD)? Choose the most appropriate one.

  1. Not remembering that a staff member greeted them 10 minutes ago.
  2. Believing they are at home when they are actually in the hospital.
  3. Walking around looking for the exit while using a day service (通所介護／デイサービス).
  4. Being unable to manage taking medicine even when it is prescribed.
  5. When encouraged to bathe, only soaking in the water and then getting out.
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 3

Why 3 is correct

Walking around looking for the exit while at a day service is behavior caused by anxiety and other feelings. This is a behavioral and psychological symptom (BPSD), so answer 3 is correct.

Why 1 is wrong

Not remembering something from a short time ago is a memory disorder (記憶障害). A memory disorder is a core symptom (中核症状), not BPSD.

Why 2 is wrong

Not knowing where you currently are is disorientation (見当識障害). This is a core symptom, not BPSD.

Why 4 is wrong

Being unable to manage medicine is caused by memory disorder or executive function disorder (遂行機能障害). This is a core symptom.

Why 5 is wrong

Not knowing the steps of bathing, such as washing the body, and only soaking in the water before getting out, is caused by a core symptom such as executive function disorder.

Key point

Walking around looking for the exit, driven by anxiety, is BPSD (行動・心理症状).

Key terms

行動・心理症状こうどうしんりしょうじょう
Behaviors and mental symptoms that appear when core symptoms combine with a person's anxiety or their surroundings. Also called BPSD.
通所介護つうしょかいご
A service where a person goes to a facility during the day for bathing, meals, and rehabilitation. Also called a day service.
