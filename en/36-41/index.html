Exam 36 · 第36回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 41 問41

認知症にんちしょう（dementia）の人ひとにみられる，せん妄もうに関かんする次つぎの記述きじゅつのうち，最もっとも適切てきせつなものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. ゆっくりと発症はっしょうする。
  2. 意識いしきは清明せいめいである。
  3. 注意機能ちゅういきのうは保たもたれる。
  4. 体調たいちょうの変化へんかが誘因ゆういんになる。
  5. 日中にっちゅうに多おおくみられる。

Translation

Among the following statements about delirium as seen in people with dementia, choose the 1 option that is the most appropriate.

  1. It develops slowly.
  2. Consciousness is clear.
  3. Attention is preserved.
  4. A change in physical condition triggers it.
  5. It is mostly seen during the daytime.
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 4

Why 4 is correct

Delirium is triggered by a change in physical condition, such as dehydration, fever, constipation, or the effects of medication. This is why, in caregiving, it is important to first check the person's physical state.

Why 1 is wrong

Delirium does not develop slowly — it starts suddenly, over a short period of hours to days. It is dementia that progresses slowly.

Why 2 is wrong

In delirium, consciousness is not clear. It becomes clouded, and the person may seem vague, getting better or worse at different times of day.

Why 3 is wrong

In delirium, attention is not preserved. Being unable to concentrate on a conversation and becoming easily distracted are features of delirium.

Why 5 is wrong

Delirium is seen more often in the evening and at night than during the daytime (this is called night-time delirium).

Key point

Delirium (せん妄) is often triggered by a change in physical condition, such as dehydration or fever.

Key terms

せん妄せんもう
A sudden disturbance of consciousness caused by a change in physical condition, causing confusion or agitation.
注意機能ちゅういきのう
The ability to direct attention to what is needed and keep concentrating on it.
誘因ゆういん
Something that triggers a symptom.
