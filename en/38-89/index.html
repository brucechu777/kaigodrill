Exam 38 · 第38回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 89 問89

Ａさん（75歳さい，男性だんせい）は，認知症にんちしょう（dementia）で，通所介護つうしょかいご（デイサービス）を利用りようしている。介護福祉職かいごふくししょくが，「今日きょうは何月何日なんがつなんにちでしょうか」と聞きくと，Ａさんは，「9月がつ」と答こたえた。介護福祉職かいごふくししょくが，「今朝けさ，Ａさんの家いえの庭にわにあじさいが咲さいていましたね」と言いうと，Ｂさんが，「あじさいが咲さくのは6月がつ？」と答こたえた。介護福祉職かいごふくししょくは，「そうです。今日きょうは6月がつ22日にちです」と言いい，ホワイトボードに日付ひづけとあじさいの絵えを描えがいた。介護福祉職かいごふくししょくが，「6月生がつうまれの方かたはいますか」と聞きくが，返事へんじがない。介護福祉職かいごふくししょくは，「今日きょうは6月がつ22日にちですが，あと3日かでＡさんの誕生日たんじょうびですね」と伝つたえた。次つぎのうち，介護福祉職かいごふくししょくが実施じっしした技法ぎほうとして，適切てきせつなものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. タッチケア（touch care）
  2. アートセラピー（art therapy）
  3. リアリティ・オリエンテーション（reality orientation）
  4. ライフレビュー（life review）
  5. グリーフケア（grief care）

Translation

Mr. A (75 years old, male) has dementia (認知症) and uses a day service (通所介護／デイサービス). A care worker asks, 'What is today's date?' Mr. A answers, 'September.' The care worker says, 'This morning, hydrangeas were blooming in Mr. A's garden.' B answers, 'Don't hydrangeas bloom in June?' The care worker says, 'That's right. Today is June 22,' and draws the date and a picture of a hydrangea on the whiteboard. The care worker asks, 'Is anyone here born in June?' but no one answers. The care worker says, 'Today is June 22, so Mr. A's birthday is in 3 days.' Which of the following is the technique the care worker used? Choose the appropriate one.

  1. Touch care (タッチケア)
  2. Art therapy (アートセラピー)
  3. Reality orientation (リアリティ・オリエンテーション)
  4. Life review (ライフレビュー)
  5. Grief care (グリーフケア)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 3

Why 3 is correct

Reality orientation (リアリティ・オリエンテーション) is a method of repeatedly sharing the date, season, place, and similar information through conversation or signs, to help a person understand reality. The care worker shared the date while talking about the season's flowers and the birthday, and also wrote it on the whiteboard, so answer 3 is correct.

Why 1 is wrong

Touch care (タッチケア) is a method of gently touching the body to give a sense of comfort. In this scene, there is no physical touch involved.

Why 2 is wrong

Art therapy (アートセラピー) is a therapy where a person expresses their feelings through art activities like drawing. Here, the care worker drew the picture only to communicate the date and season.

Why 4 is wrong

Life review (ライフレビュー) is a method of looking back on your own life and thinking about its meaning. In this scene, there is no talk of looking back on the past.

Why 5 is wrong

Grief care (グリーフケア) is care that supports the sadness of someone who has lost a person important to them. This is not related to this scene.

Key point

Reality orientation (リアリティ・オリエンテーション) repeatedly tells the date and place to help a person grasp reality.
