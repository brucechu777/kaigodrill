Exam 38 · 第38回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 85 問85

次つぎのうち，レビー小体型認知症しょうたいがたにんちしょう（dementia with Lewy bodies）の症状しょうじょうとして，最もっとも適切てきせつなものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. ノンレム睡眠行動障害すいみんこうどうしょうがいによる大声おおごえ
  2. 自律神経症状じりつしんけいしょうじょうによる起立性低血圧きりつせいていけつあつ（orthostatic hypotension）
  3. 聴覚認知機能ちょうかくにんちきのうの障害しょうがいによる幻聴げんちょう
  4. 側頭葉そくとうようの萎縮いしゅくによる意味性失語いみせいしつご
  5. 嚥下機能えんげきのうの促進そくしんによる食欲増加しょくよくぞうか

Translation

Which of the following is a symptom of dementia with Lewy bodies (レビー小体型認知症)? Choose the most appropriate one.

  1. Shouting caused by non-REM sleep behavior disorder (ノンレム睡眠行動障害)
  2. Orthostatic hypotension (起立性低血圧) caused by autonomic nerve (自律神経) symptoms
  3. Auditory hallucination (幻聴) caused by a disorder of hearing-related cognitive function
  4. Semantic aphasia caused by shrinking of the temporal lobe
  5. Increased appetite caused by improved swallowing function (嚥下機能)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 2

Why 2 is correct

In dementia with Lewy bodies, the autonomic nerves (自律神経) do not work well, so orthostatic hypotension — a drop in blood pressure when standing up — happens easily. So answer 2 is correct.

Why 1 is wrong

What is seen in dementia with Lewy bodies is REM sleep behavior disorder (レム睡眠行動障害), not non-REM sleep behavior disorder. While asleep, the person shouts or moves their body to match their dreams.

Why 3 is wrong

The key feature of dementia with Lewy bodies is visual hallucination (幻視), not auditory hallucination (幻聴). The person clearly sees people or insects that are not really there.

Why 4 is wrong

Semantic aphasia caused by shrinking of the temporal lobe is a symptom seen in semantic dementia, which is one type of frontotemporal lobar degeneration.

Why 5 is wrong

In dementia with Lewy bodies, parkinsonism (パーキンソン症状) makes swallowing function (嚥下機能) likely to get worse, not better. Care is needed to prevent choking or aspiration.

Key point

Dementia with Lewy bodies easily causes orthostatic hypotension (起立性低血圧), a drop in blood pressure on standing.

Key terms

起立性低血圧きりつせいていけつあつ
A state where blood pressure drops suddenly on standing up, causing dizziness or falling.
自律神経症状じりつしんけいしょうじょう
Symptoms caused by poor function of the nerves that control blood pressure, body temperature, bowel movements, and more.
幻聴げんちょう
Hearing voices or sounds that are not really there.
