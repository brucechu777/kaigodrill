Exam 38 · 第38回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 88 問88

次つぎの記述きじゅつのうち，認知症にんちしょう（dementia）の人ひとの遂行機能障害すいこうきのうしょうがいの症状しょうじょうとして，最もっとも適切てきせつなものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. ご飯はんを食たべたことを忘わすれて，ご飯はんをほしいと言いう。
  2. 包丁ほうちょうの使つかい方かたを忘わすれて，うまく使つかうことができない。
  3. 食事しょくじが配膳はいぜんされているが，左側ひだりがわのものを残のこす。
  4. 調理ちょうりの手順てじゅんを順序じゅんじょだてて行おこなうことができない。
  5. 皿さらの模様もようをおかずと間違まちがえて，スプーンですくおうとする。

Translation

Which of the following is a symptom of executive function disorder (遂行機能障害) in a person with dementia (認知症)? Choose the most appropriate one.

  1. Forgetting that they already ate and asking for food.
  2. Forgetting how to use a kitchen knife and being unable to use it well.
  3. Leaving the food on the left side untouched, even though the meal has been served.
  4. Being unable to carry out cooking steps in the correct order.
  5. Mistaking the pattern on a plate for food and trying to scoop it up with a spoon.
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 4

Why 4 is correct

Executive function disorder (遂行機能障害) is being unable to make a plan and carry out steps in the right order. Being unable to carry out cooking steps in order is an example of this, so answer 4 is correct.

Why 1 is wrong

Forgetting that you ate at all is memory disorder (記憶障害), not executive function disorder.

Why 2 is wrong

Being unable to use a tool correctly, even though the hands and body can move, is apraxia (失行), not executive function disorder.

Why 3 is wrong

Not noticing and leaving food on one side is hemispatial neglect (半側空間無視), not executive function disorder.

Why 5 is wrong

Being unable to correctly recognize what you see, even though your eyes can see it, is agnosia (失認), not executive function disorder.

Key point

Executive function disorder (遂行機能障害) means being unable to plan and carry out steps in order.

Key terms

遂行機能障害すいこうきのうしょうがい
Being unable to make a plan and carry out things in the correct order.
手順てじゅん
The order in which to do something.
配膳はいぜん
Serving meals: placing food on the table for a meal.
