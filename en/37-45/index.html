Exam 37 · 第37回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 45 問45

次つぎの記述きじゅつのうち，「認知症にんちしょう（dementia）の人ひとの日常生活にちじょうせいかつ・社会生活しゃかいせいかつにおける意思決定支援いしけっていしえんガイドライン」（2018年ねん（平成へいせい30年ねん）（厚生労働省こうせいろうどうしょう））で示しめされている，意思決定支援いしけっていしえんとして，最もっとも適切てきせつなものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. 認知症にんちしょう（dementia）の人ひとの家族かぞくの意思いしを支援しえんすることである。
  2. 意思決定支援者いしけっていしえんしゃは特定とくていの職種しょくしゅに限定げんていされる。
  3. 一度いちど，意思決定いしけっていしたら，最後さいごまで同おなじ内容ないようで支援しえんする。
  4. 看取みとりの場面ばめんになってから支援しえんを開始かいしする。
  5. 身振みぶりや表情ひょうじょうの変化へんかも意思表示いしひょうじとして読よみ取とる努力どりょくを最大限さいだいげんに行おこなう。

Translation

According to the 'Guidelines for Supporting Decision-Making in Daily Life and Social Life for People with Dementia (認知症)' (2018, Heisei 30, Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare), which of the following describes decision-making support (意思決定支援)? Choose the most appropriate one.

  1. It means supporting the wishes of the family of a person with dementia (認知症).
  2. The people who give decision-making support (意思決定支援者) are limited to certain job roles.
  3. Once a decision is made, the support should stay the same until the end.
  4. The support starts only when the person is close to the end of life.
  5. The support makes every effort to read gestures (身振り) and changes in facial expression as ways the person is showing their will (意思表示).
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 5

Why 5 is correct

The guideline asks supporters to make every effort to read the person's wishes even from gestures (身振り) and changes in facial expression, for people who cannot say things well in words. Decision-making support (意思決定支援) means never giving up on finding out what the person wants.

Why 1 is wrong

What is supported is always the wishes of the person with dementia (認知症), not the family. The family's words are used as a reference, but the support is not for the family's wishes.

Why 2 is wrong

Decision-making support (意思決定支援) is not limited to certain job roles. Care workers (介護福祉職), medical staff, family, and everyone around the person can give this support.

Why 3 is wrong

Feelings can change. Even after a decision is made once, supporters should check again and again, and if it changes, the support should match the new decision.

Why 4 is wrong

Support is not only for when the person is close to the end of life. It is given in everyday scenes of daily life and social life, such as meals, clothes, going out, and social activities.

Key point

Even if a person cannot say it in words, read their wishes from gestures and facial expressions.

Key terms

意思決定支援いしけっていしえん
Helping a person decide things for themselves, by giving them information and the right setting.
意思表示いしひょうじ
Showing your own thoughts or wishes to another person.
身振りみぶり
Showing feelings through movements of the hands or body.
