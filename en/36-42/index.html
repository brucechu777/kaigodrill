Exam 36 · 第36回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 42 問42

レビー小体型認知症しょうたいがたにんちしょう（dementia with Lewy bodies）にみられる歩行障害ほこうしょうがいとして，最もっとも適切てきせつなものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. しばらく歩あるくと足あしに痛いたみを感かんじて，休やすみながら歩あるく。
  2. 最初さいしょの一歩いっぽが踏ふみ出だしにくく，小刻こきざみに歩あるく。
  3. 動うごきがぎこちなく，酔よっぱらったように歩あるく。
  4. 下肢かしは伸展しんてんし，つま先さきを引ひきずるように歩あるく。
  5. 歩あるくごとに骨盤こつばんが傾かたむき，腰こしを左右さゆうに振ふって歩あるく。

Translation

Among the following, choose the 1 option that is the most appropriate gait disorder (歩行障害) seen in dementia with Lewy bodies.

  1. Feeling pain in the legs after walking for a while, and walking with rest breaks.
  2. Having difficulty taking the first step, and walking with short shuffling steps.
  3. Moving clumsily, walking as if drunk.
  4. The legs are extended straight, and the person drags the toes while walking.
  5. The pelvis tilts with every step, and the person sways the hips from side to side while walking.
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 2

Why 2 is correct

Dementia with Lewy bodies is one of the types of dementia, and it is characterized by parkinsonism (パーキンソン症状). Because of this, a person has trouble taking the first step (freezing of gait) and walks with short, shuffling steps.

Why 1 is wrong

Walking that stops because of leg pain after a while is called intermittent claudication. It is seen in diseases where the leg blood vessels narrow, or in lumbar spinal canal stenosis.

Why 3 is wrong

Unsteady, drunk-like walking is called ataxic gait. It is seen with damage to the cerebellum.

Why 4 is wrong

Walking with the legs extended and the toes dragging is called spastic gait. It is seen in conditions where the muscles become stiff, such as after a stroke or with cerebral palsy.

Why 5 is wrong

Walking where the pelvis tilts and the hips sway from side to side is called waddling gait. It is seen with hip joint disorders or muscular dystrophy, when the muscles around the hips have weakened.

Key point

In dementia with Lewy bodies (レビー小体型認知症), the first step is hard to take and the person walks with short steps (小刻み).

Key terms

レビー小体型認知症レビーしょうたいがたにんちしょう
One of the types of dementia. It is characterized by visual hallucinations, parkinsonism, and day-to-day fluctuation in condition.
歩行障害ほこうしょうがい
Difficulty walking normally. The pattern of walking differs depending on the cause.
小刻みこきざみ
Walking with very short steps, in a shuffling way.
