Exam 36 · 第36回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 44 問44

Ｌさん（78歳さい，女性じょせい，要介護ようかいご1）は，3年前ねんまえにアルツハイマー型認知症がたにんちしょう（dementia of the Alzheimer’s type）と診断しんだんされた。訪問介護ほうもんかいご（ホームヘルプサービス）を利用りようし，夫おっとの介護かいごを受うけながら二人ふたりで暮くらしている。ある日ひ，訪問介護員ほうもんかいごいん（ホームヘルパー）が訪問ほうもんすると夫おっとから，「用事ようじで外出がいしゅつしようとすると『外そとで女性じょせいに会あっている』と言いって興奮こうふんすることが増ふえて困こまっている」と相談そうだんを受うけた。Ｌさんの症状しょうじょうに該当がいとうするものとして，最もっとも適切てきせつなものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. 誤認ごにん
  2. 観念失行かんねんしっこう
  3. 嫉妬妄想しっともうそう
  4. 視覚失認しかくしつにん
  5. 幻視げんし

Translation

Ms. L (78 years old, female, care level 1) was diagnosed with dementia of the Alzheimer's type 3 years ago. She uses home-visit care (訪問介護), also known as a home help service (ホームヘルプサービス), and lives with her husband, who cares for her. One day, when a home helper (訪問介護員／ホームヘルパー) visited, her husband consulted the helper: "Whenever I try to go out on an errand, she gets agitated and says, 'You're meeting a woman outside.' It keeps happening more often, and I don't know what to do." Among the following, choose the 1 option that is the most appropriate match for Ms. L's symptom.

  1. Misidentification (誤認)
  2. Ideational apraxia (観念失行)
  3. Delusion of jealousy (嫉妬妄想)
  4. Visual agnosia (視覚失認)
  5. Visual hallucination (幻視)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 3

Why 3 is correct

Delusion of jealousy is one type of delusion seen in dementia, in which a person becomes convinced that their spouse is being unfaithful. Ms. L's behavior — becoming excited and saying, "You're meeting a woman outside," whenever her husband tries to go out — is an example of this.

Why 1 is wrong

Misidentification is mistaking a person or place for something or someone else — for example, thinking a son is one's own husband. This is different from Ms. L's case, where she is convinced her husband is being unfaithful.

Why 2 is wrong

Ideational apraxia is being unable to understand how to use tools — for example, trying to brush one's hair with a toothbrush. It has nothing to do with what Ms. L is describing.

Why 4 is wrong

Visual agnosia is being unable to recognize what something is, even though it can be seen. Ms. L recognizes her husband as her husband, so this does not apply.

Why 5 is wrong

Visual hallucination is seeing something that is not actually there. It is a symptom common in dementia with Lewy bodies. Ms. L is not "seeing" anything — she is convinced "it must be true," which is different.

Key point

Believing that your spouse is unfaithful is delusion of jealousy (嫉妬妄想).

Key terms

嫉妬妄想しっともうそう
A delusion in which a person becomes convinced that their spouse is being unfaithful. It is one type of delusion.
観念失行かんねんしっこう
Being unable to understand how to use tools, or how to carry out actions that follow a set order.
視覚失認しかくしつにん
Being unable to recognize what something is, even though it can be seen.
幻視げんし
Seeing something that is not actually there.
