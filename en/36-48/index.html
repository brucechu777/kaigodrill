Exam 36 · 第36回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 48 問48

Ａさん（80歳さい，男性だんせい，要介護ようかいご1）は，認知症にんちしょう（dementia）で，妻つまの介護かいごを受うけながら二人ふたりで暮くらしている。「夫おっとは昼夜逆転ちゅうやぎゃくてんがある。在宅介護ざいたくかいごを続つづけたいが，私わたしが体調たいちょうを崩くずし数日間すうじつかんの入院にゅういんが必要ひつようになった」と言いう妻つまに提案ていあんする，Ａさんへの介護かいごサービスとして，最もっとも適切てきせつなものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. 認知症対応型通所介護にんちしょうたいおうがたつうしょかいご（認知症対応型にんちしょうたいおうがたデイサービス）
  2. 短期入所生活介護たんきにゅうしょせいかつかいご（ショートステイ）
  3. 認知症対応型共同生活介護にんちしょうたいおうがたきょうどうせいかつかいご（認知症高齢者にんちしょうこうれいしゃグループホーム）
  4. 特定施設入居者生活介護とくていしせつにゅうきょしゃせいかつかいご
  5. 介護老人福祉施設かいごろうじんふくししせつ

Translation

Mr. A (80 years old, male, care level 1) has dementia and lives with his wife, who cares for him. His wife says, "My husband has his days and nights reversed. I want to keep caring for him at home, but my health has gotten worse and I need to be hospitalized for a few days." Among the following, choose the 1 option that is the most appropriate care service to propose to her for Mr. A.

  1. Day service for people with dementia (認知症対応型通所介護／認知症対応型デイサービス)
  2. Short-term residential care (short stay)
  3. Group home for people with dementia (認知症対応型共同生活介護／認知症高齢者グループホーム)
  4. Specified facility residential care
  5. Long-term care welfare facility for the elderly (介護老人福祉施設)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 2

Why 2 is correct

Short-term residential care (short stay) is a service where a person stays at a facility for just a few days and receives care there. Mr. A can be looked after only for the days his wife, the caregiver, is in the hospital, and once she is discharged, care at home can continue as before — this fits the wife's wishes.

Why 1 is wrong

A day service for people with dementia is a service where a person attends a facility only during the day and returns home at night. This cannot cover the few days when his wife is hospitalized and no one is at home. Mr. A also has his days and nights reversed.

Why 3 is wrong

A group home for people with dementia is a place where a person moves in and lives permanently. It is not for using just a few days, and it does not match the wife's wish to continue home care.

Why 4 is wrong

Specified facility residential care is a service received after moving into somewhere like a fee-based elderly home. This would also mean moving Mr. A's home, so it is not suited to a short-term stay.

Why 5 is wrong

A long-term care welfare facility for the elderly (also called a special nursing home for the elderly) is for long-term admission, and in principle it is for people at care level 3 or above. Mr. A is at level 1, so this does not fit for that reason too.

Key point

Staying a few days to receive care is short-term residential care (短期入所生活介護, short stay).

Key terms

短期入所生活介護たんきにゅうしょせいかつかいご
Short stay. A service where a person stays at a facility for a short period and receives care.
認知症対応型通所介護にんちしょうたいおうがたつうしょかいご
A service where a person with dementia attends a facility only during the day and receives care.
特定施設入居者生活介護とくていしせつにゅうきょしゃせいかつかいご
A care service received by a person who has moved into somewhere like a fee-based elderly home.
介護老人福祉施設かいごろうじんふくししせつ
Long-term care welfare facility for the elderly, also called a special nursing home for the elderly. A facility for long-term admission, in principle for people at care level 3 or above.
