Exam 36 · 第36回 · 認知症の理解 (Understanding Dementia)

Question 46 問46

バリデーション（validation）に基もとづく，認知症にんちしょう（dementia）の人ひとの動うごきや感かん情じょうに合あわせるコミュニケーション技法ぎほうとして，正ただしいものを1つ選えらびなさい。

  1. センタリング（centering）
  2. リフレージング（rephrasing）
  3. レミニシング（reminiscing）
  4. ミラーリング（mirroring）
  5. カリブレーション（calibration）

Translation

Among the following, choose the 1 option that is the correct communication technique, based on validation, for matching the movements and emotions of a person with dementia.

  1. Centering (センタリング)
  2. Rephrasing (リフレージング)
  3. Reminiscing (レミニシング)
  4. Mirroring (ミラーリング)
  5. Calibration (カリブレーション)
Show answer and explanation

Answer: 4, 5

Why 4, 5 is correct

This question has two correct answers. Option 4, mirroring, is a technique of matching your own movements to the other person's movements and expressions, moving in the same way. Option 5, calibration, is a technique of matching your own feelings to the other person's emotions, tuning in to how they feel. Since both are techniques for "matching movements and emotions," both 4 and 5 were accepted as correct.

Why 1 is wrong

Centering is a preparation the caregiver does before starting to interact — steadying their own breathing and focusing their mind. It is not a technique for matching the other person.

Why 2 is wrong

Rephrasing is a technique of repeating back the person's own words exactly as they said them. What is matched here is the words, not movements or emotions.

Why 3 is wrong

Reminiscing is a technique of having the person talk about old memories. It draws out memories from the past, and does not match the person's present movements or emotions.

Key point

Mirroring (ミラーリング) matches the other person's movements; calibration (カリブレーション) matches their feelings.

Key terms

バリデーションバリデーション
A communication method for interacting with a person with dementia by accepting their words and emotions as they are.
ミラーリングミラーリング
A technique of matching the other person's movements and expressions, moving the same way like a mirror.
カリブレーションカリブレーション
A technique of matching your own feelings to the other person's emotions, feeling the same way.
